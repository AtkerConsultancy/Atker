Privacy and cookies

A short, plain explanation of what this site records and how to switch it off.

What we use

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to count visits and see which pages, links and social posts bring people here. It only runs if you click Accept on the cookie notice. If you click Decline, no analytics cookies are set and nothing is sent to Google Analytics.

What is recorded

Pages viewed, how you arrived (for example a link from Instagram or Pinterest), roughly where in the world you are, your device and browser type, and which buy buttons you click. We do not collect your name or email through analytics, and we do not use the data for advertising.

Contact forms

Forms on this site open an email to us with the details you typed. Nothing is stored on the website itself.

Links to other sites

Buy buttons take you to Amazon or Gumroad (sold through our partner, Digital Revenue Studio). They have their own privacy and cookie policies. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

Change your choice

Open cookie settings to accept or decline again at any time.
